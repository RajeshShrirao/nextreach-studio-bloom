"Website" covers ten genuinely different products. They are not interchangeable, they cost very different amounts, and picking the wrong one is the most common expensive mistake a Pune business makes.

This guide explains all ten: what each one does, what it costs, and when it is genuinely the right answer.

## 1. Landing Page

**What it is:** a single page focused on one offer, one audience, one action. Not a homepage with sections.

**When it works:** you have a specific offer, a specific traffic source, and a specific action. Google Ads campaigns, a new service launch, a single product.

**When it does not:** you have five services and no clear priority. You do not need five landing pages - you need a proper site with service pages.

Our [landing page guide](/blog/landing-page-design-conversion/) covers the structure.

## 2. Brochure Site

**What it is:** 5-10 marketing pages. About, services, contact, maybe a portfolio. No database, no user accounts, minimal forms.

**The most common website in the Pune market, and the most over-sold.** A brochure site is legitimate when your business is genuinely local, your customers find you by word of mouth or Google Business Profile, and the site exists to confirm you are real.

**It is the wrong choice if** you want to rank for service keywords, because one page per service is what ranking requires - and a brochure site usually has one page about everything.

**Honest Pune verdict:** ₹20,000-₹60,000 for this is correct. Paying more is waste.

## 3. Lead Capture Site

A brochure site plus forms that actually work, connected to WhatsApp, email, and ideally your CRM.

**The upgrade that matters most.** Most Pune sites have a form that submits and delivers nothing. Fixing that is worth more than any design change. Our [SME priorities guide](/blog/website-design-pune-sme-priorities/) covers how to test it.

## 4. Content Platform

A site with a real publishing system: blog, guides, comparison pages, FAQs, structured data.

**This is the SEO play.** If you want to rank for informational queries, this is the structure that supports it. Every service page supported by a cluster of related articles, all internally linked.

**Requires:** a CMS you can use, an editorial commitment, and someone who will actually publish. A content platform nobody writes for is an expensive empty blog.

**This is also where AI answer visibility comes from.** Analysis of 1,000 frequently AI-cited pages found short paragraphs, lists, and question-and-answer formatting were the consistent structural patterns - and analysis of 1,000 pages cited by AI answers found those same patterns plus specific attributed statistics. Our [AI search guide](/blog/ai-search-local-seo-pune-aeo-geo/) covers the approach.

## 5. Corporate Site

Brand-heavy, multi-section, leadership content, often multilingual. Common for larger Pune firms and manufacturing groups.

**Justified when** the site is a primary business development asset and brand perception genuinely matters - enterprise sales, large B2B contracts, international operations.

**Often over-applied.** A ₹2,00,000 corporate site for a 10-person local business is usually a brochure site with a bigger invoice.

## 6. E-Commerce Store

Products, cart, checkout, payment gateway, inventory, shipping. See the [full platform comparison](/blog/ecommerce-website-design-pune/).

**Costs range enormously** because catalog size, pricing complexity, and integrations drive everything. ₹35,000 for a 100-product Shopify store to ₹8,00,000+ for a multi-vendor marketplace.

**India-specific requirements that are not optional:** UPI, GST invoicing, COD handling, WhatsApp order updates.

## 7. Booking Site

Availability, scheduling, payment, reminders. Common for clinics, salons, service businesses, showrooms.

**Usually the right type for Pune service businesses** with appointment-based revenue. Not a brochure site pretending to take bookings - a real system that prevents double-booking.

**The question to ask:** can a customer book at 2am without you? If the answer requires a phone call during working hours, you are losing bookings.

For clinics specifically, our [AI receptionist guide](/blog/ai-receptionist-clinics/) covers extending this to answer calls and handle intake.

## 8. Web Application

Login, database, user roles, business workflows, integrations. The most expensive and most valuable type.

**Justified when** your business logic is genuinely unusual - approval chains, custom pricing, multi-tenant SaaS, ERP integration, operational workflows no platform models.

**Not justified for** a marketing site, regardless of how ambitious it sounds. See [custom versus WordPress](/blog/custom-website-vs-wordpress-pune/) for the honest cost framing.

**This is where AI agents fit.** An AI agent needs somewhere to live, data to access, and permissions to hold - which means a web application or an integration into an existing one. Our [AI agent service page](/services/ai-agent-development-pune/) covers that build.

## 9. Portfolio Site

Showcase work with minimal copy. For photographers, architects, designers, consultants, contractors.

**The one type where being quiet works.** Big images, minimal text, a clear contact path. Costs ₹15,000-₹60,000 and typically pays for itself through credibility alone.

## 10. Directory or Listing Site

Searchable listings with user submissions, reviews, categories, geographic filtering.

**Genuinely large projects** - ₹2,00,000 and up. Justified for marketplaces, local directories, supplier networks, franchise finders.

**The hardest type to do well**, because you are building a two-sided platform: you need listings (supply) and visitors (demand), and the sequencing problem is real. Most Pune directory projects start with neither.

## How to Choose: A Decision Path

Answer in order and stop at your first "yes".

1. **Do you need people to log in and see their own data?** → **Web application**
2. **Do you need to sell products online?** → **E-commerce store**
3. **Do customers need to book an appointment?** → **Booking site**
4. **Do you need to rank for informational searches across many topics?** → **Content platform**
5. **Do you have exactly one offer and one audience for one campaign?** → **Landing page**
6. **Is your work the product?** → **Portfolio site**
7. **Is your business large enough that brand perception is a sales tool?** → **Corporate site**
8. **Otherwise** → **Brochure site**, ideally as a lead capture site with working forms

## The Honest Default for Most Pune SMEs

For a typical Pune small and medium business - service-based, local customers, ₹20,000-₹80,000 budget - the right answer is a **lead capture site** with these properties:

- 5-8 pages, one per service
- Mobile-first, passing Core Web Vitals
- A tap-to-chat WhatsApp button above the fold
- Forms that notify you on WhatsApp
- LocalBusiness and Service structured data
- A maintained Google Business Profile
- Real photography of your actual business
- Content you can actually publish yourself

That covers roughly 90% of what a Pune SME needs. It costs ₹35,000-₹90,000 to build and ₹15,000-₹40,000 a year to run.

**Spend the difference on a booking system or an e-commerce store once revenue justifies it.** The expensive mistake is spending the budget on a corporate site that does the same job more slowly.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How much does a small business website cost in Pune?**
₹20,000-₹60,000 for a brochure site, ₹35,000-₹90,000 for a lead capture site with working forms and integrations, ₹50,000-₹1,50,000 for a content platform. Full breakdown in our [Pune website cost guide](/blog/website-design-cost-pune-2026/).

**What is the most common type of business website?**
The brochure site - 5 to 10 marketing pages with minimal functionality. It is also the most frequently over-engineered and under-invested.

**Do I need a custom website or is a template fine?**
For types 2, 3, and 4, templates are genuinely fine. For types 6, 7, and 8, custom or platform-specific work is necessary. The decision is about what your site must do, not about prestige.

**Is a website necessary for a very small Pune business?**
If you get all your customers through referrals and WhatsApp, you need a Google Business Profile and a single landing page. That is the honest answer at very small scale. A full site becomes necessary the moment you want to be found on search.

**How many pages does a small business website need?**
One per service, plus about, contact, and home. Five to eight pages is usually right. More only if each page targets a genuinely distinct search intent.

**Which website type is best for ranking on Google?**
Content platform, because it supports many specific queries rather than competing for one. Second is a lead capture site with one page per service. A single-page brochure site is the weakest option for search.

**Can I change website type later?**
Yes, and it is common. A brochure site that grows into a content platform is straightforward. A brochure site that suddenly needs user accounts is a rebuild. Plan for type 8 early if you might need it.
