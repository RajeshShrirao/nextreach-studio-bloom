India has one of the highest mobile traffic shares in the world at roughly 80%, and over 70% of e-commerce sales happen on mobile. That single fact should drive every decision in your store design - yet most Pune e-commerce sites we audit were designed on a 27-inch monitor and checked on a laptop.

This guide covers the three realistic platform choices, what each actually costs, and the decisions that are expensive to reverse.

## Shopify: The Right Default

For most Pune businesses launching their first online store, Shopify is the correct answer.

**Why it works:** no server management, automatic security updates, hosted checkout, and an app ecosystem that covers most requirements without custom code. Payments, inventory, discounts, and shipping are handled. You launch in weeks rather than months.

**Pune-specific fit:** the ₹2,000/month platform fee is roughly what most businesses would spend on developer maintenance anyway. For a first store, that is a good trade.

**The honest limitations:**

- **Transaction fees** beyond payment gateway charges. They add up at volume.
- **Checkout cannot be deeply customised.** Shopify Plus unlocks more, at a higher tier.
- **Monthly rent does not stop.** This is a real ongoing cost that often gets omitted from Pune e-commerce quotes.
- **Content-heavy stores** are awkward. Shopify is a commerce platform, not a publishing platform.

**Choose Shopify when** you are launching a first store, your catalog is under roughly 1,000 SKUs, your pricing is straightforward, and you want to be selling in under a month.

## WooCommerce: Ownership With Maintenance

WooCommerce is a WordPress plugin. That single fact explains both its appeal and its cost.

**Why it works:** no platform rent, full code ownership, and complete design freedom. If you already run WordPress, it fits naturally. B2B stores with customer-specific pricing, bulk order logic, or credit accounts are far easier here than on Shopify.

**The honest limitations:**

- **You own the security burden.** WordPress powers a large share of the web and is correspondingly targeted. Updates are not optional.
- **Performance requires discipline.** WordPress passes Core Web Vitals on only 38.4% of sites - the lowest rate of any major CMS. Plugin bloat is the usual cause.
- **Checkout abandonment is your problem to solve.** You are responsible for payment gateway integration, fraud screening, and PCI compliance.
- **It needs a technical owner.** Someone who can maintain it.

**Choose WooCommerce when** you already run WordPress, you need B2B pricing logic, you want no monthly platform fee, and you have someone responsible for maintenance.

## Custom and Headless: When It Is Genuinely Worth It

Headless commerce separates the frontend from the backend. You get complete control over the customer-facing experience and complete responsibility for it.

**Legitimate reasons to go custom:**

- Catalogue above roughly 5,000 SKUs with complex faceted navigation
- Multi-vendor marketplace with seller onboarding and payouts
- Complex B2B pricing: customer-specific rates, credit terms, quotation workflows
- ERP, CRM, or warehouse integration that no plugin handles
- Performance requirements where load time is directly tied to revenue at volume
- A genuinely distinctive buying experience that templates cannot produce

**The honest warning.** A 50-product boutique store built custom will never outperform a good Shopify store, and will cost three times as much to maintain. Custom architecture is justified by complexity, not by ambition.

Field data shows Astro-based sites pass Core Web Vitals on 48% of the web versus 29.3% for Next.js. A custom headless build using Astro is a defensible choice when performance genuinely matters to your revenue.

## The India-Specific Requirements

These are not optional for a Pune store.

**Payment gateways.** Razorpay, PayU, CCAvenue, PhonePe, and UPI. UPI dominates Indian checkout - if your store does not offer it, you will lose carts to competitors who do. Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Shopify Pay where appropriate.

**GST invoicing.** Automatic GST calculation and compliant invoice generation. This is mandatory, and manually generating invoices at scale is unworkable.

**Shipping and COD.** Cash on delivery remains significant for Indian consumers. Handle COD reconciliation properly - returns are the main failure mode.

**WhatsApp integration.** For Pune businesses, order confirmation and status updates over WhatsApp outperform email. Many customers will not read an email.

**Hinglish UX.** Indian customers frequently switch between scripts. Do not force English-only interfaces.

## Design Decisions That Matter More Than Platform

Platform choice is maybe 30% of the outcome. These are the rest.

**Mobile-first checkout.** Over 70% of your traffic is mobile. Build the checkout on a 390px screen first. Address input, payment selection, and the final CTA all need to work one-handed.

**Product imagery.** Roughly 90% of shoppers say product images are the most important factor in an online purchase. Budget for real photography. For Pune D2C brands this is the highest-leverage line item in the whole project.

**Page weight.** The median web page weighs 2.6MB across 71 requests. A store with unoptimised product images will exceed 3MB quickly. Lazy-load everything below the fold.

**Structured data.** Product schema enables rich results. Combined with correct inventory, this is how you appear in AI shopping answers - see our [AI search guide](/blog/ai-search-local-seo-pune-aeo-geo).

**Analytics from day one.** GA4 plus conversion tracking, and a record of what your baseline is. Without a baseline you cannot prove the redesign worked.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Shopify or WooCommerce better for a Pune business?**
Shopify for a first store, D2C, and simplicity. WooCommerce if you already run WordPress, need B2B pricing logic, or want no monthly fee. Most Pune D2C brands should start on Shopify.

**How much does an e-commerce website cost in Pune?**
₹35,000-₹75,000 for a starter store with up to 100 products. ₹75,000-₹2,00,000 for a growth store with integrations and CRO. ₹2,00,000+ for custom B2B or marketplace builds.

**Do I need a custom e-commerce website to compete?**
No. Platform choice rarely decides the outcome. Product photography, page speed, checkout friction, and shipping reliability decide it. A well-built Shopify store beats a poorly executed custom build every time.

**Should I migrate from WooCommerce to Shopify?**
Only if maintenance burden is the real constraint and you have outgrown platform flexibility. Migrations are disruptive - product, customer, order history, and SEO authority all move. Migrate when the platform actively blocks you, not when it becomes inconvenient.

**How do I reduce cart abandonment?**
UPI first, guest checkout, minimal form fields, visible shipping costs early, and WhatsApp order confirmation. Address abandonment data specifically - most Indian cart abandonment is at the address and payment steps.

**Do I need a mobile app for my store?**
Not initially. A responsive store plus WhatsApp ordering will outperform an app for most Pune businesses. Apps matter for repeat-purchase frequency or loyalty, and only once you have the traffic to justify them.

**What is headless commerce and do I need it?**
Separating frontend from backend for complete design and performance control. You need it if you have genuine complexity or performance requirements. A small store does not.
