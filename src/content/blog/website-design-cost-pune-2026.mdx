## The ₹9,999 Website Package: Why Cheap Quotes in Pune Cost 4x Later

Every week, business owners across Baner, Balewadi, and Kharadi contact our studio after a demoralising experience with a "cheap" Pune web agency. The pattern is almost identical: they accepted an advertisement promising an "All-Inclusive Business Website at ₹6,999 or ₹9,999 with Free Domain, Hosting & SEO in 7 Days."

Here is what that ₹9,999 quote actually buys behind the marketing curtain:

1. **A Pirated or Free Stock Template:** The agency does not design anything. They download a pre-built Elementor or WordPress theme, swap your logo, paste unformatted text, and deliver it. You receive zero unique visual identity.
2. **Shared ₹99/Month "Free Hosting":** Your site is crammed onto an oversold shared server alongside 5,000 other domains. The Time to First Byte (TTFB) exceeds 1.8 seconds, causing mobile visitors to bounce immediately.
3. **Core Web Vitals Failure:** Across 37,036 tested sites, **52% of page-one Google results fail Core Web Vitals**, with 67% failing on Largest Contentful Paint (LCP). Stock WordPress templates on cheap hosting pass Core Web Vitals on only 38.4% of builds. Google demotes these pages in competitive local SERPs.
4. **"Free SEO" Is an Empty Plugin:** Agencies claim to include "Complete SEO Setup." In reality, they install a free plugin like Yoast, leave the meta descriptions blank, and generate zero structured JSON-LD schema.
5. **The Hostage Model:** Once launched, you do not own your source code. When you request a minor text revision, the vendor charges ₹2,000 per change or stops answering your phone calls entirely.

**The Financial Reality:** Within six to twelve months, the business owner discovers their ₹9,999 website does not rank on Google, fails to generate a single inquiry, and has been flagged for malware due to unpatched WordPress plugins. They end up paying ₹40,000 to ₹80,000 to a credible engineering studio for a complete ground-up rebuild. The ₹9,999 site ended up costing ₹70,000+ in wasted capital, lost leads, and rebuild costs.

## What AI Genuinely Cut From Pricing

The generative AI boom fundamentally altered software development economics, but not in the way aggressive marketing pitches claim:

- **Design exploration is 70% faster:** Iterating layouts, color palettes, and component states that previously required two weeks of wireframing now happens in two days using AI-assisted design systems.
- **Boilerplate engineering is automated:** Writing contact form validation, responsive flexbox/grid containers, and standard API wrappers costs virtually nothing in engineering hours.
- **Micro-copy scaffolding is immediate:** Drafting first-pass meta descriptions, FAQs, and structural content outlines takes minutes rather than days.

### What AI Did NOT Cut

1. **Strategic Positioning:** An LLM cannot determine why a manufacturing buyer in Chakan values export quality certifications over price discounts.
2. **Business Logic & Custom Integrations:** Connecting an inventory database to Razorpay and a custom dispatch system still demands experienced human software engineers.
3. **Quality Assurance & Performance Auditing:** Google DORA research confirmed that unvetted AI-generated code correlates with a 10% increase in software instability. Every deployed line requires human verification.
4. **Real Operating Infrastructure:** Domain registration (₹800–₹1,500/year), cloud infrastructure, SSL renewal, and reliable content delivery networks cost the same regardless of how code was written.

The net effect: AI reduced the build cost of a standard 8-page business website by roughly **25% to 35%**, not 90%. Anyone offering a ₹3,000 "AI-built website" is generating unvalidated slop that will damage your commercial brand.

## Questions to Ask Any Pune Web Agency Before Signing

Before transferring a deposit to any web development agency in Pune, demand written answers to these five technical criteria:

1. **"Do I hold 100% unencumbered ownership of the source code and repository?"** If the vendor hosts the site on their personal account and gives you only an editor login, you do not own your website.
2. **"What is the guaranteed Core Web Vitals score on a mid-range 4G mobile phone?"** Demand a contractual commitment to pass Google's Largest Contentful Paint (LCP < 2.5s) and Interaction to Next Paint (INP < 200ms).
3. **"What exact schema markup will be injected into my pages?"** Ask whether they inject LocalBusiness, Service, BreadcrumbList, and FAQPage JSON-LD.
4. **"What happens when something breaks six months from now?"** Clarify bug-fix windows, hosting maintenance fees, and technical support SLAs in writing.
5. **"Can you share three live production URLs you built in Pune that pass Google PageSpeed right now?"** Check the live URLs yourself on `pagespeed.web.dev`.

Cheap packages almost always exclude domain renewals, SSL certificates, business email accounts, plugin licensing fees, and ongoing maintenance. Within twelve months, fixing slow load times, malware compromises, and poor mobile layouts often costs ₹30,000 to ₹60,000 in emergency redesign expenses.

**Is WordPress or a modern framework like Astro cheaper to run long-term?**
A modern static framework like Astro is significantly cheaper long-term. In contrast, maintaining a secure, fast WordPress site typically requires ₹12,000 to ₹25,000 annually in managed hosting and security maintenance.

**What should be included in a legitimate Pune website development contract?**
A credible contract must include 100% source code ownership, full mobile responsiveness, on-page SEO meta tags, Google Search Console and Analytics integration, structured JSON-LD schema markup, at least two rounds of design revisions, and a minimum of 30 days of post-launch technical warranty.
