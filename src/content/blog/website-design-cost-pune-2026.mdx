Ask ten Pune agencies what a business website costs and you will get ten numbers spanning two orders of magnitude - from ₹3,000 to ₹3,00,000+. That is not a sign of a dishonest market. It is a sign that nobody has explained what the number buys.

This guide breaks down real 2026 pricing, what AI genuinely changed about it, and what it did not.

## What Each Price Tier Actually Buys

### ₹8,000 - ₹20,000: Template Websites

A pre-built WordPress or Wix theme with your logo and content. Mobile responsive, 3-5 pages, contact form, basic on-page SEO.

Fine for: freelancers and very early-stage businesses who need to exist online.
Not fine for: anything requiring conversion optimisation or competitive differentiation. This tier produces the sites that take 58% of Pune businesses into a redesign within 18 months.

### ₹20,000 - ₹60,000: Standard Business Websites

Custom design matching your brand, 5-10 pages, Google Maps integration, contact forms, on-page SEO setup, WhatsApp CTA.

This is the sweet spot for most Pune SMBs and the band we would put most clients in. If a vendor quotes you ₹4 lakh for a 6-page service website, ask what they are actually building.

### ₹60,000 - ₹1,50,000: Custom-Designed Websites

Designed from scratch to reflect your brand specifically. Custom UI/UX, advanced functionality, performance optimisation, comprehensive on-page SEO.

Ideal for growing businesses in competitive Pune sectors - education, healthcare, real estate - where the website is doing commercial work rather than existing.

### Above ₹1,50,000: Custom Web Applications

This is no longer a website. Custom dashboards, user authentication, role-based access, database architecture, third-party API integrations, payment flows.

At this point you are buying engineering, not design, and the relevant pricing guidance is our [custom software development cost breakdown](/blog/cost-custom-software-development-india).

## What AI Genuinely Cut

The AI website builder market hit an estimated $6.3 billion in 2026, up roughly 26% year over year, with projections reaching $20.1 billion by 2033 at a 31.1% compound growth rate. SME adoption leads, at around 63% of the market.

Here is what that money actually bought:

**1. The design phase compressed.** Layout exploration that took a week now takes a day. AI design agents - which profile a business and build around real content rather than filling a template - grew roughly 340% year over year in adoption. For a Pune business owner, the practical effect is that getting to a decent visual concept is cheaper and faster than it was two years ago.

**2. Boilerplate is free.** Forms, validation, responsive grids, API routes, basic auth. This was always cheap but is now effectively zero.

**3. Content drafts are free.** First-pass copy, meta descriptions, FAQ scaffolding. With heavy caveats - see below.

**4. Motion became cheap.** AI-generated motion content costs roughly 5-10% of equivalent traditional video production. A hero animation that used to be a line item is now nearly free.

**5. Short-form builds got genuinely fast.** AI-assisted builds compress a 3-5 page brochure site from three weeks to a few days. Our own launch tiers are built this way - 1-2 days for a one-page site, 3 days for a bespoke flagship.

## What AI Did Not Cut

This is the part the marketing ignores.

**Strategy is not automated.** Someone still has to decide what your site should say, to whom, and what action it should drive. AI cannot infer that your customers in Aundh care about turnaround time more than price.

**Custom development is not automated.** Your competitors' scheduling system has logic specific to their operation. AI will not produce it.

**Testing and QA are not automated.** 43% of AI-generated code changes need additional debugging after deployment. Google DORA research found AI adoption correlated with roughly 10% higher code instability. Someone has to do this.

**Content is not automated meaningfully.** Generic AI copy actively hurts. It reads as filler because it is filler. Specificity - your actual processes, your actual numbers, your actual customers - still requires knowing your business.

**Ongoing costs are unchanged.** Domain ₹600-₹1,500 per year. Shared hosting ₹2,000-₹5,000 per year, or ₹8,000-₹15,000 for managed WordPress with real traffic. SSL should be free via Let's Encrypt. Maintenance ₹1,500-₹5,000 per month.

Add it up: ongoing costs for a small business website run roughly ₹15,000-₹40,000 per year after the initial build. That is ₹1,250-₹3,300 a month. It catches people off guard because they budgeted only for the build.

**And the trap:** a ₹20,000 site often ends up costing ₹80,000 in fixes and lost business within the first year. That is the number vendors who do this work are judged on.

## Why Cheap Hosting Destroys Cheap Websites

One warning that applies more in Pune than most markets: ₹99/month shared hosting is the most common avoidable cause of Core Web Vitals failure, and Core Web Vitals affect both rankings and conversion.

The current data makes the cost of this concrete. Across 37,036 websites, 52% of page-one Google results fail Core Web Vitals, and among failing pages, 67% fail on Largest Contentful Paint. WordPress - by a wide margin the most common choice at this budget tier - passes Core Web Vitals on only 38.4% of sites.

A fast site on good hosting beats a well-designed site on cheap hosting. This is not theoretical; it is the single most common technical failure we find auditing inherited sites. Our [Core Web Vitals guide](/blog/website-speed-core-web-vitals-pune) covers what to check.

## What Actually Determines Your Quote

**Page count and uniqueness.** A 5-page brochure site costs a fraction of a 30-page service website. Each genuinely distinct page adds design, build, and content time.

**Custom versus template.** Templates use pre-built themes. Custom means designing from scratch.

**Integrations.** Payment gateway, WhatsApp Business API, CRM sync, booking system. Each one adds meaningful engineering.

**Content volume and source material.** Do you have photography and copy ready, or does it need writing?

**Who maintains it after launch.** A site nobody can update is a liability within a year.

## Questions That Reveal Everything

Ask these before accepting any quote:

1. **What framework is this built on, and do I own the source code?** If the answer is "you get access to the Wix/Shopify account" rather than a code repository, you are renting.
2. **What are the target Core Web Vitals on a mid-range Android phone?** A real number or "we optimise for speed" tells you everything.
3. **What accessibility standard?** WCAG 2.2 AA should be a named deliverable.
4. **What happens when something breaks at 11pm?** Who is on call and what does the contract say?
5. **Can I see a site you built that is live right now?** Not a portfolio mockup - a live URL.

If a vendor cannot answer question 5, the ₹3,000 quote and the ₹3,00,000 quote are both equally meaningless.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How much does a business website cost in Pune in 2026?**
₹20,000-₹60,000 for a professional 5-10 page site from a Pune agency. ₹15,000-₹30,000 for a basic template build. ₹1,50,000 and above once you need custom functionality or a database. Most Pune SMBs should budget ₹30,000-₹60,000 for a site that generates enquiries.

**Is a 20,000 rupee website bad?**
Not necessarily, but understand what you bought: a template with your logo. It will be fine for showing you exist. It will struggle to outrank a competitor with a properly built site on good hosting, and it will likely need replacing within two years.

**Should I use an AI website builder instead of hiring anyone?**
For a service business that needs lead capture, an AI builder is now genuinely sufficient and cheap. Choose custom development when you need a booking system, customer portal, payment flow, or integration with your existing tools. See [vibe coding versus hiring a developer](/blog/vibe-coding-vs-hiring-a-developer) for where the boundary is.

**What is the cheapest way to get a website that ranks on Google?**
A static site on a fast framework with proper on-page SEO. Astro passes Core Web Vitals on 48% of sites versus WordPress at 38.4%. You do not need a large budget - you need the right architecture and honest hosting.

**How much should I budget for website maintenance annually?**
Roughly ₹15,000-₹40,000 per year for hosting, domain, backups, and small updates. Budget this from day one. Unmaintained sites get slow and get compromised.

**Do AI-generated websites cost less to build?**
The build is somewhat cheaper, mostly in the design phase and boilerplate. Strategy, custom logic, testing, and ongoing costs are unchanged. The realistic saving on a standard business website is 20-30%, not 80%.
