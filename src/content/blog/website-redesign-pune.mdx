Pune businesses average a website redesign roughly every 2.8 years, and the industry estimate is that over 58% of businesses that built sites cheaply under ₹10,000 redesigned within 18 months.

That second statistic explains the first. Most redesigns are not planned improvements. They are responses to a site that no longer works.

This guide covers how to work out which situation you are in, what a redesign should actually cost, and how to avoid losing your search rankings during one.

## Repair or Rebuild: The Honest Test

Run these six checks in order. The first failure usually determines the answer.

| Check | Pass | Diagnose | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Core Web Vitals** | LCP < 2.5s, INP < 200ms, CLS < 0.1 | 52% of page-one Google results fail these. If yours does too, speed is the problem | Repairable on most sites |
| **Mobile usability** | No horizontal scroll, forms work, tap targets ≥24px | Frequently a theme problem on WordPress | Usually repairable |
| **Content structure** | Every service has its own page | Many Pune sites still bury everything on one page | Structural rebuild |
| **Search equity** | Rankings stable or rising | If traffic declined after a Google update, something broke | Diagnose first |
| **Technical debt** | Page weight under 1.5MB, plugin count under 15 | Plugin-stacked WordPress is the usual culprit | Rebuild often cheaper |
| **Business fit** | Site reflects what you sell today | Most sites are 3-5 years behind the business | Rebuild |

**The pattern:** speed, mobile, and technical debt are repairable. Content structure and business fit require a rebuild.

## Signals You Need a Rebuild Rather Than a Fix

- The site was built on WordPress with 30+ plugins and nobody knows what half of them do
- Everything lives on one long scrolling homepage
- You cannot publish a page without emailing your developer
- The design predates mobile-first and was never updated
- Your service lines have changed substantially since launch
- Content cannot be found by search engines on the site at all
- The business now needs login, database, or booking - none of which existed before

## Signals You Only Need Repairs

- The site looks dated but works
- Core Web Vitals are failing on image weight
- A few forms are broken
- There is no structured data
- The content is fine, the plumbing is not

**This is far more common than agencies admit.** A redesign proposal for a site that only needs image optimisation and a schema pass is a proposal for a sale, not a diagnosis.

## What a Redesign Should Cost in Pune

| Scope | Pune range | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| Visual refresh, same structure | ₹15,000 - ₹40,000 | 1-2 weeks |
| Full redesign, same platform | ₹30,000 - ₹80,000 | 3-5 weeks |
| Redesign + content restructure + CMS | ₹60,000 - ₹1,50,000 | 4-8 weeks |
| Redesign + new platform + integrations | ₹1,20,000 - ₹3,00,000 | 6-12 weeks |
| Rebuild to custom web application | ₹1,50,000+ | 8-20 weeks |

Two lines that get hidden in most proposals:

**Content migration.** If your existing site has 40 pages, someone has to move and check every one. Budget ₹10,000-₹30,000 if the content is being reorganised rather than copied.

**Redirect mapping.** Every old URL needs a corresponding new URL. Missing redirects are the single most common cause of traffic loss after a redesign. This should never be an afterthought - see below.

## Protecting SEO Across a Rebuild

This is where most Pune redesigns go wrong. You lose rankings you spent three years building.

**Keep every URL identical wherever possible.** The strongest protection is not changing URLs. If the structure must change, you are committing to a migration budget.

**Build a complete redirect map before you launch.** Every old page to its new equivalent. Include 301s, not 302s - a 302 is temporary and Google treats it as such.

**Do not drop content that has inbound links.** Find your top linked pages first. If a page earns external links, it survives the redesign.

**Keep your title tags and meta descriptions** unless there is a specific reason to change them. They are not design.

**Preserve your structured data.** Do not let a designer remove your schema because it clutters the markup view.

**Launch to a staging URL first.** Verify indexing, redirects, and analytics before pointing the domain.

**Expect a 4-8 week recovery window** after a structural migration. Rankings dip while Google re-crawls. If you cannot tolerate a dip, a cosmetic refresh is safer than a structural rebuild.

## The Questions That Reveal Whether a Vendor Has Done This Before

1. **What is your redirect strategy?**
A competent vendor answers with a mapping process. An inexperienced one says "Google will figure it out." It will not.

2. **Will the site be down during the migration?**
It should not be. Staging build, then DNS switch. If the answer involves downtime, ask why.

3. **How do you handle content that has no equivalent in the new structure?**
There is always an honest answer here. Redirecting a service page to your homepage is a ranking loss the vendor should disclose.

4. **What happens to my current rankings during the switch?**
Any vendor who promises "no drop" is not being honest. A small dip is normal.

5. **Can you show me a before-and-after on a client site?**
Including the traffic data. Not just screenshots.

## A Practical Sequence for Pune SMEs

**Week 1 - Measure.** Core Web Vitals, current traffic, current rankings, top linked pages. Get the data before anyone proposes anything.

**Week 2 - Diagnose.** Repair, restructure, or rebuild. Write it down. Reject any proposal that does not start from a diagnosis.

**Week 3-4 - Decide.** If it is a repair, do the repair. Do not let a repair become a rebuild because the designer found the colour palette tiring.

**Week 5+ - Execute.** If it is a rebuild, content first, then design, then build, then migrate with full redirects.

The most expensive outcome is a redesign that solves a problem you did not have while introducing one you did.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How often should a business website be redesigned?**
Every 2.5-3 years is the Pune average, but that is reactive rather than optimal. Redesign when the business changes, not when the design feels old. If your service lines and customers are the same, the design does not need replacing.

**Is a redesign worth it or should I start fresh?**
If your current site is structurally sound, a redesign beats a rebuild. If it is on a bloated platform with a plugin problem, a rebuild is often cheaper than the redesign.

**How much does a website redesign cost in Pune?**
₹30,000-₹80,000 for a full redesign on the same platform. ₹60,000-₹1,50,000 with content restructuring and a proper CMS. Our [full Pune cost breakdown](/blog/website-design-cost-pune-2026) covers what each tier includes.

**Will a redesign hurt my Google rankings?**
A cosmetic refresh on the same URLs should not. A structural migration will cause a temporary dip, typically 4-8 weeks, if redirects are correct. Bad redirects can cause a permanent loss. This is the highest-risk part of any redesign.

**Should I redesign before or after fixing SEO?**
Fix technical SEO first - speed, mobile, structured data. Then redesign. Otherwise you are rebuilding a site with the same underlying problems. Our [structured data playbook](/blog/schema-markup-structured-data-playbook) and [Core Web Vitals guide](/blog/website-speed-core-web-vitals-pune) cover both.

**Can I keep my current domain during a redesign?**
Yes, and you should. Domain history carries authority. Change platforms freely; do not change domains.

**How long does a website redesign take in Pune?**
A visual refresh is 1-2 weeks. A full redesign with restructuring is 4-8 weeks. Anything claiming a full redesign in three days is a template swap.
