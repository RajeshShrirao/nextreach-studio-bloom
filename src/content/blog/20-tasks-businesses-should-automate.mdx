Every business owner knows they should automate more. But knowing *what* to automate is harder than knowing *that* you should.

This list covers 20 specific tasks across 5 business functions. Each one is something we've automated for real Pune businesses in the past year. They're ranked by two criteria: how easy they are to set up (effort) and how much time or money they save (ROI).

Use this as your automation checklist. Pick 3. Start this week.

## Finance & Accounting

### 1. Invoice Generation (Effort: Low, ROI: High)

When a sale is recorded, the invoice should already exist. Manual invoice creation is one of the biggest time wasters in small businesses.

**What to automate:** Connect your billing software (Tally, Zoho Books, or even Google Sheets) to an invoice generator. When an order is marked "complete," auto-generate a PDF invoice and email it to the customer.

**Time saved:** 5-10 hours per month per 100 invoices.

### 2. Expense Report Categorization (Effort: Medium, ROI: Medium)

Employees send receipt photos via WhatsApp or email. Someone manually sorts them into categories - travel, supplies, meals - and enters them into accounting software.

**What to automate:** Use AI to read receipt images, extract the amount, vendor, date, and category, and post the entry directly to your accounting software.

**Time saved:** 8-12 hours per month for a 20-person team.

### 3. Overdue Payment Reminders (Effort: Low, ROI: High)

Chasing late payments is awkward and time-consuming. Most businesses don't do it consistently, which means money sits uncollected.

**What to automate:** When an invoice is 7 days past due, send a polite email reminder. At 15 days, send a WhatsApp message. At 30 days, escalate to a phone call alert for the finance team. [We build these workflows](/services/ai-automation-pune) for Pune businesses starting at ₹45K.

**Time saved:** 3-5 hours per month + 15-25% faster payment collection.

### 4. Monthly P&L Report Generation (Effort: Medium, ROI: Medium)

Someone in your finance team spends 2-3 days every month pulling reports, formatting them, and emailing them to stakeholders.

**What to automate:** Connect your accounting software to a reporting tool or Google Sheets. Generate the P&L, balance sheet, and cash flow statement automatically on the 1st of every month.

**Time saved:** 2-3 days per month.

## Sales

### 5. Lead Enrichment (Effort: High, ROI: High)

When a leads come in from your website or WhatsApp, they often have just a name and phone number. Your team spends time Googling them to find company name, industry, and role.

**What to automate:** Connect a lead enrichment tool (like Apollo or Lusha) to your CRM. When a lead is created, auto-fill missing fields from public databases.

**Time saved:** 3-5 minutes per lead. At 50 leads/week, that's 4+ hours.

### 6. Follow-Up Sequencing (Effort: Low, ROI: Very High)

Most salespeople follow up once. If the prospect doesn't reply, the lead goes cold. Follow-up sequences increase reply rates by 3x-5x.

**What to automate:** Set up a sequence of 3-5 follow-ups at defined intervals - WhatsApp on day 1, email on day 3, call alert on day 7, LinkedIn message on day 14. Each message adds value (case study, testimonial, offer update).

**Time saved:** Amazingly, this is usually zero setup effort in most CRMs. It's just rarely configured.

### 7. Proposal Status Tracking (Effort: Low, ROI: Medium)

Salespeople send proposals and wait. They check in manually, waste time, and often miss the optimal follow-up window.

**What to automate:** Track when a proposal is opened. If it's opened 3+ times, alert the salesperson: "Your proposal to ABC is being reviewed - they've opened it 3 times today. Call now."

**Time saved:** 2-3 hours/week + higher close rates.

### 8. Quote Generation (Effort: Medium, ROI: High)

Every quote requires copy-pasting pricing, checking stock, adding taxes, and formatting.

**What to automate:** Set up a quote template system. Salesperson selects line items → system checks current pricing and stock → generates a professional PDF quote → emails it to the customer and logs it in the CRM.

**Time saved:** 15-20 minutes per quote. At 20 quotes/week, that's 6+ hours.

## Marketing

### 9. Email Welcome Sequences (Effort: Low, ROI: High)

Someone signs up for your newsletter or downloads a guide. Do they get an email immediately? If not, you're losing engagement while they're interested.

**What to automate:** Set up a 4-5 email welcome sequence in your email tool (Mailchimp, MailerLite, or even Gmail + sheets). Day 1: Welcome + what to expect. Day 3: Best content. Day 7: Case study/testimonial. Day 14: Offer/intro call.

**Time saved:** 5-10 hours/month + 40% higher engagement rates.

### 10. Social Media Scheduling (Effort: Low, ROI: Medium)

Posting daily on LinkedIn, Instagram, and Twitter/X is a full-time job if done manually.

**What to automate:** Batch create 2 weeks of content in one sitting. Schedule it using a tool like Buffer, Later, or Hootsuite. Never post manually again.

**Time saved:** 5-8 hours per week.

### 11. Analytics Report Distribution (Effort: Medium, ROI: Low)

Someone pulls Google Analytics data, formats it into a dashboard, and emails it around.

**What to automate:** Set up Google Looker Studio (free) or a weekly automated email from GA4. Stakeholders get the numbers in their inbox every Monday morning.

**Time saved:** 2-4 hours per week.

### 12. Review & Testimonial Collection (Effort: Low, ROI: High)

Happy customers rarely leave reviews unless asked. Manual follow-up is inconsistent.

**What to automate:** After a purchase or project completion, auto-send a Google review link via WhatsApp or email. If no review in 7 days, send a reminder. If the customer had a negative interaction, route to a manager instead.

**Time saved:** 2-3 hours/week + 3x more reviews.

## HR & People

### 13. Employee Onboarding (Effort: High, ROI: High)

Every new hire means the same manual steps: create email, add to payroll, order laptop, schedule orientation, introduce to team.

**What to automate:** When a new hire is added to the HR system, trigger a sequence: create email account (automated via Google Workspace API), add to Slack channels, schedule calendar events for orientation and IT setup, send welcome email with docs.

**Time saved:** 4-6 hours per new hire. For a company hiring 2-3 people/month, that's 8-18 hours.

### 14. Leave Request Processing (Effort: Low, ROI: Medium)

Leave requests go back and forth via email or WhatsApp. Managers approve manually. HR updates a spreadsheet.

**What to automate:** A simple form → manager gets notified → approve/reject with one click → leave balance auto-updates in the HR system → team calendar shows who's out.

**Time saved:** 1-2 hours per week.

### 15. Timesheet Reminders (Effort: Low, ROI: Low)

Someone chases employees for timesheets every week. Or weekly timesheets don't happen at all.

**What to automate:** Auto-send a Google Form or Toggl reminder every Friday at 4 PM. If not submitted by Monday 10 AM, send a reminder to the employee's manager.

**Time saved:** 1-2 hours per week.

### 16. Expense Approval Workflow (Effort: Medium, ROI: Medium)

Receipts come in, someone reviews them, forwards to the approver, waits, follows up.

**What to automate:** Receipt → auto-categorize with AI → route to the right approver based on amount and department → if no approval in 48 hours, send an escalation alert.

**Time saved:** 3-5 hours per week.

## Operations

### 17. Inventory Low-Stock Alerts (Effort: Medium, ROI: High)

Stock runs out. Nobody noticed until a customer order can't be fulfilled.

**What to automate:** Set a minimum stock threshold for each product. When inventory drops below it, auto-send an email/WhatsApp to the procurement team and optionally generate a purchase order.

**Time saved:** Prevents stockouts - the cost of lost sales from a single stockout can cover the automation 10x over.

### 18. Order Confirmation & Tracking (Effort: Low, ROI: High)

Customer places an order. They immediately want to know: "Did you get it? When will it arrive?"

**What to automate:** Order received → auto-send WhatsApp confirmation with order number → when shipped, auto-send tracking link → on delivery, auto-request a review.

**Time saved:** 5-10 hours/week + higher customer satisfaction.

### 19. Daily Report Generation (Effort: Low, ROI: Medium)

Someone compiles yesterday's numbers - orders, revenue, issues - and emails them to the management group.

**What to automate:** Connect your POS, CRM, or operations software to a reporting tool. Auto-generate and email a daily summary at 8 AM.

**Time saved:** 1-2 hours/day.

### 20. Compliance Document Generation (Effort: High, ROI: High)

Weekly, monthly, or quarterly compliance reports require pulling data from multiple systems, formatting it, and submitting it.

**What to automate:** Connect your data sources to a document generation tool. Auto-fill compliance templates and generate PDFs that just need a final review before submission.

**Time saved:** 5-10 days per quarter for compliance-heavy industries (manufacturing, logistics, healthcare).

## Where to Start

You don't need to do all 20. Pick 3 based on your biggest pain point:

- **If collections are slow:** Start with invoice automation + overdue reminders
- **If sales are inconsistent:** Lead enrichment + follow-up sequencing
- **If your team feels overwhelmed:** Onboarding automation + expense reporting

For each task, ask: "How much time does this take per week? What's it costing us?" If the answer is 5+ hours and ₹10K+/month, automate it.
