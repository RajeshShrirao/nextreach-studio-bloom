Now let's break down what each tier actually looks like.

## Tier 1: Simple Automation (₹30K - ₹80K)

These are single-task automations that replace one manual process. They connect two tools or automate one repetitive action.

**Real examples we've built:**

- **WhatsApp order notifications** for a restaurant: When a new order comes through Zomato/Swiggy, automatically send a confirmation to the customer and a prep alert to the kitchen team. ₹35K. Built in 5 days.
- **Email-to-Slack forwarding** for a logistics company: When a customer sends a tracking inquiry email, create a Slack alert for the ops team with the order details. ₹30K. Built in 3 days.
- **Google Sheets → WhatsApp invoice reminders** for a freelancer: Check a sheet daily, find unpaid invoices past due, and send a polite WhatsApp reminder. ₹45K. Built in 1 week.

**Best for:** Small businesses, freelancers, and single-department workflows where one repetitive task is eating 5-10 hours per week.

**ROI math:** If a data entry person costs ₹15K-₹25K/month, a ₹45K automation pays for itself in 2-3 months.

## Tier 2: Medium Automation (₹80K - ₹2L)

These involve multiple steps, conditional logic, and integration between 2-3 systems. They replace an entire process, not just one task.

**Real examples:**

- **CRM-to-WhatsApp pipeline** for a real estate agency: When a lead is added to Zoho CRM, auto-send a property brochure via WhatsApp. If the lead opens it, schedule a follow-up. If they reply, tag them for the sales team. ₹1.2L. Built in 3 weeks.
- **Invoice + inventory automation** for a wholesale distributor: When a sale is recorded in the billing software, auto-generate an invoice in PDF, email it to the customer, update inventory counts across 3 warehouses, and trigger a reorder alert if stock falls below threshold. ₹1.8L. Built in 4 weeks.
- **HR onboarding flow** for a 50-person company: When a new hire is added to the HR system, auto-create their email account, Slack profile, add them to the payroll sheet, schedule orientation, and send them a welcome packet. ₹1.5L. Built in 3 weeks.

**Best for:** Growing SMEs that have outgrown manual processes across multiple departments. If you have 2-3 people doing data entry and cross-system syncs, this is your tier.

**ROI math:** Replacing 2 full-time data entry roles (₹30K-₹50K/month combined) means an ₹1.5L automation pays for itself in 3-5 months. Plus fewer errors.

## Tier 3: Complex Automation (₹2L - ₹5L+)

These are multi-system integrations involving AI components - document processing, natural language understanding, decision logic, and dashboards.

**Real examples:**

- **AI document processing** for a logistics firm: Invoices, packing lists, and delivery receipts arrive as PDFs and images. AI extracts key fields (PO number, amount, date, vendor), validates against the ERP system, flags discrepancies, and posts approved entries automatically. ₹3.8L. Built in 6 weeks.
- **End-to-end order management** for a manufacturing company: Orders come in via email, WhatsApp, and web portal. AI routes them to the right production line, auto-generates work orders, schedules raw material procurement, and sends status updates at every stage. ₹4.2L. Built in 8 weeks.
- **Multi-channel customer support automation** for an e-commerce brand: AI handles 70% of incoming queries across email, WhatsApp, and web chat. Escalates complex issues to human agents with full context. Built-in analytics dashboard. ₹3.5L. Built in 5 weeks.

**Best for:** Mid-size businesses, manufacturers, and companies processing high volumes of structured or semi-structured data. If you have 5+ people doing manual data work across departments, this is where you get the biggest ROI.

**ROI math:** Harder to calculate cleanly, but clients typically see 40-60% reduction in processing time and 80% reduction in data entry errors. One logistics client went from 3 days to 4 hours for monthly invoice reconciliation.

## Hidden Costs to Watch For

- **Tool subscriptions**: Some automations require paid API access (WhatsApp Business API, OpenAI credits, Zapier premium). Budget ₹2K-₹15K/month ongoing.
- **Maintenance**: Expect ₹5K-₹20K/month for maintenance after the first 6 months, depending on complexity.
- **Training**: Your team needs 2-4 hours of training to use and monitor the system. Factor this into your rollout timeline.

## When Not to Automate

Not everything is worth automating:

- **Processes that change monthly** - You'll spend more on maintenance than you save
- **Low-volume tasks** (< 10 hours/month) - The setup cost rarely beats manual effort
- **Tasks requiring human judgment** - AI is good at pattern recognition, bad at context-dependent decisions
