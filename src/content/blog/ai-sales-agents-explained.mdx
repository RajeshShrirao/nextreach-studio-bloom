## The Sales Agent Workflow

A modern AI sales agent follows a five-step process that mirrors what a good human SDR (sales development representative) does - but at machine speed and scale.

### Step 1: Prospect Research

The agent starts with a target profile. "Find Pune-based logistics companies with 20-200 employees that are still using spreadsheets for fleet management." It searches LinkedIn, company websites, news mentions, and directories. For each prospect, it builds a profile: company size, recent funding or news, the decision-maker's role and background, and a trigger event if one exists (hiring spree, new office, new competitor raised funding).

This research happens in seconds per company, not hours.

### Step 2: Personalized Email Writing

For each prospect, the agent writes a unique email - not a template with a merge field, but genuinely personalized copy. It references the prospect's recent LinkedIn post, mentions a challenge common to their industry, and explains exactly how your service helps.

The LLM behind the agent (typically GPT-5, Claude 4, or a fine-tuned Llama 4 model) generates natural, human-sounding copy that passes spam filters and doesn't trigger the "this is AI" instinct.

### Step 3: Sending and Tracking

The agent sends emails through your existing email infrastructure (Google Workspace, Outlook, or a dedicated sending domain). It staggers sends to avoid spam flags, warms new domains automatically, and tracks opens, clicks, and replies.

### Step 4: Reply Handling

When a prospect replies, the agent reads the response and decides what to do. A positive reply ("Tell me more") gets a follow-up with a calendar link. A negative reply ("Not interested") gets a polite sign-off and moves to the bottom of the queue. A neutral reply ("Send me some info") gets relevant case studies or pricing.

The agent can handle full conversations - answering objections about pricing, timeline, or fit. When a conversation gets beyond its scope (a technical question about integration specifics, for instance), it flags a human to step in.

### Step 5: Meeting Booking

When a prospect agrees to a meeting, the agent checks your calendar, sends available times, and books directly. The meeting appears on your calendar with the prospect's profile, the conversation history, and relevant notes attached. You walk in prepared.

## What Technology Powers This?

Under the hood, an AI sales agent is a pipeline of four components:

**Large Language Model (LLM)** - The brain. Handles research analysis, email writing, reply comprehension, and decision-making. Most production agents use a combination of a fast model (for classification and decisions) and a capable model (for email generation).

**CRM Integration** - The memory. The agent reads from and writes to your CRM (HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho, or a custom system). It knows which leads have been contacted, what stage they're in, and never reaches out to the same company twice.

**Email API** - The delivery mechanism. Handles send limits, bounce handling, domain reputation monitoring, and reply-forwarding.

**Orchestration Layer** - The workflow manager. Decides who to contact when, what to say, how many follow-ups to send, and when to escalate to a human.

## Common Concerns - Addressed

**"Will it sound robotic?"**

Old AI outreach sounded robotic because it used simple template-based generation. Modern agents using capable LLMs produce emails that pass the "would I reply to this?" test. The key is proper prompt engineering and a well-written knowledge base about your business. A poorly configured agent sounds like a spam bot. A well-configured one sounds like a good salesperson.

**"What about spam laws?"**

AI sales agents comply with CAN-SPAM and Indian IT Act regulations. They include a physical address, a working unsubscribe link, accurate subject lines, and they only send to B2B prospects where there's a legitimate business interest. Most agents also check the Do Not Disturb (DND) registry before sending.

**"Will this replace my sales team?"**

No. It replaces the repetitive, manual parts of outbound - the prospecting, the research, the follow-up chasing. Your sales team still does the actual selling: discovery calls, demos, negotiations, closing. The agent fills the top of the funnel so your humans can focus on the bottom.

## Who Should Use AI Sales Agents?

AI sales agents work best for businesses with:
- A clear ICP (ideal customer profile) that can be described precisely
- Sales cycles under 3 months
- Outbound as a primary or secondary channel
- A CRM with clean data
- At least one person who can review and refine the agent's output weekly

They work less well for businesses selling to very small lists of enterprise accounts (where every outreach is deeply relationship-driven) or businesses where the buyer needs heavy education before they're ready to talk.
