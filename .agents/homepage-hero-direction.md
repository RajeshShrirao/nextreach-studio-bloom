# Homepage hero direction

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** The first viewport should make “Reach what’s next” felt as a visible movement from a compact origin to a resolved destination. It refuses the standard agency hero of a centered pitch over a decorative mockup.

**OWN-WORLD:** A near-black editorial field, thin warm-white measurement marks, restrained peach/amber signal light, crisp Cabinet display type, and small mono coordinates that behave like useful annotations. The visual is open and atmospheric rather than boxed, glossy, or neon.

**STORY:** A visitor understands in seconds that NextReach moves businesses forward by building the digital systems that get them there. They can start a project or move directly to the work without reading an explanation.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** The left half carries `NEXTREACH STUDIO`, `Reach what’s next.`, the supplied one-line subhead, and two clear actions. A continuous Three.js scene occupies the right half and bleeds into the copy: a signal path leaves a small origin low-left, rises through sparse nodes and coordinate fragments, and terminates at a luminous `N →` destination. On mobile, the copy and CTAs come first, followed by the same scene as a tall, touchable canvas.

**FORM:** A procedural trajectory instrument: Catmull-Rom path geometry, a shader-driven pulse and soft atmospheric points, plus a compact destination identity mark. It is the user-pinned visual metaphor and remains the single authored moment. Seed key: `2cb32dae` (degraded roll; no catalog challengers were available).

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
