# Long-Tail Keyword Map — Pune Web Development

**Strategy:** ignore head terms, own the specific questions Pune businesses actually type.

**Audited:** 2026-10-05 · commit `cd6258f` (live) · 43 blog · 2 guides · 2 resources

**Why long-tail and not head terms:** head terms in this market are unwinnable. Your own
SEO plan records 586 agencies competing, with IKF at 1,500 clients over 25 years and
Dimakh at 1,500 designs over two decades. A newer domain loses on age regardless of
content quality.

Long-tail is different in three specific ways:

- **Lower competition.** "Website redesign cost in Pune" has real volume and a fraction of
  the competition of "web design company in Pune".
- **Higher intent.** Someone searching "Core Web Vitals Pune" or "how much does a website
  cost in Pune" is close to buying. Someone searching "web design Pune" is browsing.
- **It maps to your advantage.** A technical studio with real Core Web Vitals data beats an
  SEO agency on performance topics, and beats a ₹4,999 template shop on cost transparency.

## The wiring problem, restated

Ranking is not the constraint. **22 of 43 articles have zero inbound links from commercial
pages.** The long-tail map above identifies roughly 20 keywords you could target. You
already hold articles for about half of them, and most of those articles are not linked
from the pages that convert.

Two things, in order:

1. **Wire the orphans.** Zero new content. Unblocks revenue pages you already published.
2. **Then write the 10 above**, industry-first.

Doing both at once would be a mistake — new articles land on pages that already fail to
pass authority to the ones you have.
