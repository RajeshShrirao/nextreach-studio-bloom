# 11. WhatsApp Objection Handling Cards

Copy-paste responses for the 6 most common objections when selling websites on WhatsApp.

> **House rule for every card:** talk about risk, outcome and trust — not technology. No speed-test scores, no "4x faster", no framework names, no unsubstantiated guarantees. Technical details are proof you bring out only if asked, never the headline.

### Objection 1: "Another agency / freelancer quoted ₹2,000–₹3,000 on WordPress."
> **Core Answer:** Risk + outcome + professionalism. Be willing to lose a bad fit.

```text
Absolutely — there are cheaper options available. 👍

If your only goal is to get any website online, I'd actually recommend taking the cheaper option.

Where we differ is that we focus on making the website something you can confidently send to customers — professional design, a good mobile experience, a clear enquiry flow, and a clean handover where you own everything.

For your business, I'd rather recommend the smallest package that gets that job done properly.
```

---

### Objection 2: "Can you do it in 2 days instead of 3?"
> **Core Answer:** Yes, with fast asset handover — in first person.

```text
Yes 👍 If you can send the logo, business details and photos today, I can prioritize the build and aim to have the first version ready within 48 hours.

Shall we confirm the details and start? 🚀
```

---

### Objection 3: "Are there any hidden monthly or yearly costs?"
> **Core Answer:** One-time project, you own it, no mandatory retainer.

```text
No surprise agency fees. 👍

Your website is a one-time project.

You own the website and source code, and there is no mandatory monthly agency retainer.

The only ongoing third-party cost is normally the domain renewal, which you pay directly in your own name.
```

---

### Objection 4: "What if I don't like the design or want changes?"
> **Core Answer:** You review a private preview before anything goes live + 30-day window. (Use this before they object, not just after.)

```text
You're covered on this. 🤝

1. I send you a private preview link within 48–72 hours.
2. You open it on your phone and tell me what you'd like adjusted — colors, text, images.
3. We revise it, and it only goes live on your domain once you're satisfied.
4. Plus a 30-day window after launch for minor updates at no extra charge.
```

---

### Objection 5: "I don't have text or photos ready right now."
> **Core Answer:** You don't need a finished brief — we structure it with you.

```text
No problem 👍 You don't need to arrive with a finished website brief.

Send us your business name, services and whatever photos or information you already have. We'll help structure the content and fill in the gaps so you don't have to write the entire website yourself.
```

---

### Objection 6: "Do you handle the domain connection and launch setup?"
> **Core Answer:** Yes — launch handled for you, registered in your name.

```text
Yes, that's all handled for you. 🌐

Whether you already own a domain or need help getting one, we take care of connecting it and launching the site. Everything stays registered in your name, so you keep full control.

You just look at the preview, approve it, and watch it go live. 🚀
```
