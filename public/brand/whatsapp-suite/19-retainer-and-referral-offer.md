# 19. Post-Launch Retainer & Referral Offer

Send this after the website is live and the client has confirmed that everything works.

## Maintenance option

```text
Your website is live 🎉

If you would like us to keep it updated after the included 30-day support period, we can handle small changes for ₹999/month:

• Up to 2 small content or image updates per month
• Link, phone number, and WhatsApp CTA checks
• Basic uptime and deployment support
• Monthly analytics snapshot when tracking is connected

This is optional. You own the website and can manage it yourself. Reply MAINTENANCE if you want us to reserve ongoing support.
```

Do not promise unlimited edits. Larger changes, new pages, SEO campaigns, and new integrations should be quoted separately.

## Referral request

```text
One small favour, if you’re happy with the launch: do you know one local business that needs a clean website and direct WhatsApp enquiries?

You can introduce us in a WhatsApp group or share their number only if they are comfortable being contacted. If they book a website sprint, we’ll send you a ₹1,000 thank-you credit or apply it to your next update.
```

Record the referral source in the lead sheet before contacting the referred business.
