# 17. Fast Website Sprint — Client Agreement Template

Use this as a plain-language sign-off in WhatsApp or as the basis for a PDF agreement. Replace every `{{placeholder}}` before sending.

```text
FAST WEBSITE SPRINT AGREEMENT

Client: {{ClientBusinessName}}
Contact: {{ClientName}}
Provider: NextReach Studio / Rajesh Shrirao
Package: {{PackageName}}
Project price: ₹{{TotalAmount}}
Expected delivery: {{DeliveryDays}} working days from confirmed kickoff

1. SCOPE
NextReach Studio will design, build, test, and deploy the agreed website package described in the approved quote. Work outside that scope, including additional pages, custom dashboards, e-commerce, booking logic, copywriting beyond the agreed amount, or new integrations, will be quoted separately before work begins.

2. PAYMENT
The client pays 50% (₹{{DepositAmount}}) to reserve the sprint. The remaining 50% (₹{{BalanceAmount}}) is due after the approved website is ready for launch. The website, domain transfer, and final handover are released after the balance is received.

3. CLIENT INPUTS
The client will provide accurate business information, logo files, photos, service details, contact numbers, and domain access when requested. Delivery dates move if required assets, approvals, or access are delayed.

4. REVISIONS
The package includes one consolidated round of reasonable text, image, and spacing refinements against the approved direction. New direction or scope changes are quoted separately.

5. OWNERSHIP
After full payment, the client receives the website source code and agreed design assets. Third-party fonts, stock media, plugins, hosting accounts, and external subscriptions remain subject to their own licenses and fees.

6. SUPPORT
The project includes 30 days of complimentary support for minor text, image, link, or contact-number corrections. New features, redesigns, new pages, and ongoing content work are separate engagements.

7. APPROVAL
The client approves this scope, price, payment schedule, and timeline by replying “APPROVED” on WhatsApp and sharing the payment confirmation.
```
