## Pipeline stages

1. **New** — lead came from an ad, referral, form, or outbound list.
2. **Contacted** — welcome message sent; waiting for a reply.
3. **Replied** — the lead has responded, but the need is not clear yet.
4. **Qualified** — business, package, timeline, and decision-maker are known.
5. **Quote sent** — scope and price sent; next follow-up date is mandatory.
6. **Payment pending** — client agreed in principle; deposit instructions sent.
7. **Won / onboarding** — deposit received and intake checklist sent.
8. **Lost / nurture** — no fit, no response after the final follow-up, or delayed decision.

Never leave a lead without a `Next action date`. A lead without a next action is a forgotten lead.

## Daily operating rhythm

### Morning — 30 minutes

- Reply to every new WhatsApp message before starting delivery work.
- Move every conversation to the correct stage.
- Set the next action date for every active lead.
- Send the first follow-up to leads due today.

### Acquisition block — 60 to 90 minutes

- Publish or launch the planned ad creative.
- Review yesterday's clicks, WhatsApp starts, qualified leads, and sales.
- Contact the daily prospecting quota if paid volume is low.

### Delivery block

- Keep active client work inside the existing onboarding and handoff checklists.
- Send one proactive progress update per active sprint.

### Evening — 15 minutes

- Clear WhatsApp replies and quote questions.
- Mark wins and losses with a reason.
- Confirm tomorrow's follow-up queue.

## Starter targets

Use these as operating targets for the first two weeks, then replace them with your own observed numbers:

```text
New paid conversations per day: 5–10
First response time during business hours: under 15 minutes
Qualified leads per week: 5
Quotes sent per week: 3
Closed website sprints per week: 1–2
Follow-ups completed on due date: 100%
```

The goal is not to make every number look good. The goal is to locate the bottleneck:

- Clicks but no WhatsApp starts → creative, landing page, or CTA problem.
- WhatsApp starts but no replies → welcome message or trust problem.
- Replies but few qualified leads → offer or qualification problem.
- Quotes but few deposits → proof, scope, payment, or follow-up problem.
- Deposits but late launches → capacity or delivery system problem.

## Minimum lead review

At the end of each week, calculate:

```text
WhatsApp start rate = WhatsApp CTA clicks / landing page sessions
Qualification rate = qualified leads / replied leads
Quote rate = quotes sent / qualified leads
Close rate = won leads / quotes sent
Average order value = total won revenue / number of wins
Cost per qualified lead = ad spend / qualified leads
Cost per sale = ad spend / won leads
```

Record the result by campaign and creative. Do not judge an ad only by cheap clicks.

## Handoff rule

When a payment is received, update the lead to `Won / onboarding`, record the amount and date, create the project folder, and send:

1. `13-client-onboarding-document.md`
2. `07-requirement-form.md`
3. The agreed scope and delivery date

When the site is live, send `14-project-handoff-document.md`, request a review, and ask whether the client knows one other business that needs the same sprint.
