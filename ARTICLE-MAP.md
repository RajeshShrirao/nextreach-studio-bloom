which gaps remain before we can credibly rank for web development searches in Pune.

---

## The short answer

Keyword clustering works. It already has. But **topic domination does not yet** — and the
reason matters more than the article count.

Six of the seven clusters below can realistically dominate. One (Dev Tooling) cannot, and
should not try. It serves a developer audience that has no Pune intent, so it earns domain
authority and backlinks rather than Pune traffic. That is a legitimate job, just a
different one — it is the SEO flywheel described in `MARKETER-GUIDE.md`, not a local play.

## Two honest observations

**Topic domination on "web design in Pune" is not winnable head-on.** IKF claims 1,500
clients over 25 years, Dimakh 1,500 designs over two decades, Savit and SPEORA both
incumbent SEO firms. A new domain competing for the generic head term head-on would lose on
domain age regardless of content quality.

What the clusters above *can* do is dominate the long tail — "responsive web design Pune",
"local SEO Pune", "website design cost Pune", "Core Web Vitals Pune" — which is where
Pune businesses actually search and where a technically credible studio has an advantage
over an SEO agency. The clusters are built for that, not for "web design Pune".

**Publish dates matter for these.** Several new articles share late-2026 dates. Google
treats a batch of 19 posts appearing simultaneously as less trustworthy than a steady
cadence. Consider staggering further updates — and genuinely refreshing older articles —
rather than publishing another 19 at once.
