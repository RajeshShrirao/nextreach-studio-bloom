# NextReach Studio — One-page strategic brief

**9 October 2026 · Proposed 90-day direction, subject to paid validation.**

**Who we are:** A founder-led website design and development studio in Chakan/Pune. Clear business communication, practical implementation and direct accountability.

**Who we serve:** Owner-led industrial suppliers and small manufacturers with a live reason to improve their website: buyer outreach, a new capability, a trade event or an unusable existing site. Start with one repeatable supplier list. Test independent physiotherapy clinics as a bounded challenger; industrial superiority is not established.

**The problem:** Buyers cannot quickly understand what a capable business supplies, whether it is a fit, and how to enquire. Information is scattered across conversations, documents and a weak website.

**Positioning:** We organize a business's capabilities into a clear website that buyers can evaluate and use to request a quote.

**Why choose us:** A focused capability interview, approved technical information, a practical enquiry route, one accountable founder and documented handover. Demonstrate these commitments; do not claim an exclusive competitive advantage.

**Our promise:** Agreed scope. Working preview. Tested enquiry route. Client ownership. No promised number of leads, rankings or revenue.

**Our message:**

- Tagline: **Ready for your next buyer.**
- Headline: **Websites that show buyers what you can deliver.**
- Supporting copy: “Bring your capabilities, product details and business story together in a clear, mobile-friendly website. Work directly with the founder, agree the scope before building, and keep ownership after launch.”
- Primary CTA: **Get a website scope.**
- Secondary CTA: **See an example.** Label concepts conspicuously.

**How we look and sound:** Calm, specific, approachable and precise. Keep the ascent emblem, terracotta/cream/charcoal palette, Cabinet Grotesk and Plus Jakarta Sans. Deep rust for accessible functional text/buttons. Genuine work imagery, clear specification layouts, restrained motion. Premium means care and clarity—not complex visuals or “elite engineering” language.

**What we sell first:** A Capability Website, proposed at ₹18,000 for a bounded pilot: five named pages, limited content help, standard enquiry routing, two consolidated review rounds and handover. Test movement toward ₹24,000 with evidence. Preserve ₹5,000 one-page launches for content-ready buyers only if the scope and hours work. Disclose hosting, renewals, taxes and exclusions.

**How customers find us:** Warm introductions and targeted local outreach first. Use one relevant example and a matching landing page. Maintain accurate eligible GBP information; reuse actual project evidence on LinkedIn. Ads and more content follow qualification and delivery evidence.

**What we will not do:** Fabricate proof, disguise demos as clients, sell everything equally, promise lifetime-free infrastructure, accept unlimited revisions, guarantee growth, or add AI/apps by default.

**The operating test:** Repeated paid work, collected contribution per founder hour, useful delivery and genuine feedback—not conversation counts or another rebrand.
