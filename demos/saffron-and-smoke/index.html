01 — Our Philosophy

Where Ancient Sigri Meets Precision Technique

“We do not tamper with nostalgic flavours; we build a stage worthy of their depth.”

— Chef Kabir Roy, Culinary Director

At Saffron & Smoke, Indian dining is emancipated from heavy gravies and artificial theatrics. Founded in 2024 in Baner, Pune, our culinary atelier honors the centuries-old North Indian hearth—infusing native woods, charcoal embers, and Kashmiri saffron into modern, sculptural compositions.

Every spice is hand-sourced at origin: Mongra saffron from the terraces of Pampore, organic black urad simmered for 36 hours over babool wood charcoal, and cold-pressed mustard oil churned in stone kolhus. We respect time as our primary ingredient.

01 / HEAT

Sigri & Charcoal

Natural fruitwood coals imparting subtle, non-overpowering smoky undertones.

02 / ORIGIN

Pampore Saffron

Grade-A Mongra strands steeped in chilled spring water for uncompromised aroma.

03 / TIME

36-Hour Simmer

Slow artisanal reductions without cornstarch, food coloring, or shortcuts.

03 — The Space

An Architectural Sanctuary in Baner

Conceived in collaboration with leading spatial designers, the interiors of Saffron & Smoke draw inspiration from the raw basalt stone of the Deccan plateau, paired with hand-hammered copper, dark fluted wood, and recessed amber illumination.

No blaring background music or intrusive service. The dining room offers whispering acoustic privacy, an open hearth where embers glow in full view, and bespoke seating designed for unhurried dinners that stretch long into the night.
