The Glass House & Ocean Pavilion

Timeline
18 Months Turnkey
Recognition
AD100 Best Villa 2025

Architectural Concept & Site Response

Perched on a rocky beachfront bluff overlooking the Arabian Sea, The Glass House was conceived as a light, floating teak-and-concrete pavilion that touches the sensitive coastal earth with minimal intervention.

A 7-meter dramatic post-tensioned concrete roof cantilevers toward the sea without a single corner column, allowing the floor-to-ceiling Schuco motorized sliding glass facades to completely disappear into hidden wall pockets. During winter and evening hours, the living pavilion transforms into an open-air breezeway cooled by natural sea air.

The 25-meter infinity lap pool is cladded with midnight-black flamed basalt, creating a mirror that reflects the changing colors of the coastal sunset.

Material Palette

  • Facade: Board-marked C40 structural concrete
  • Timber: Reclaimed Burma Teak louvers
  • Flooring: Monolithic Silver Grey Travertine
  • Glazing: Low-iron acoustically laminated glass
  • Fixtures: Antonio Lupi & Boffi custom joinery
