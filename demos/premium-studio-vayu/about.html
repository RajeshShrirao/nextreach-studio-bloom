Our Manifesto

Contextual Architecture That Breathes

Vayu (वायु) translates to the vital breath of wind in Sanskrit. We design living environments that harness natural air currents, seasonal solar trajectories, and regional materials.

A 16-Year Dialogue Between Modernity & Indian Craft

Founded in 2010 by master architects Devang Singhania (AA London) and Tanya Mehta (CEPT Ahmedabad), Studio Vayu began with a simple conviction: luxury is not excess decoration; luxury is proportion, natural daylight, unhurried space, and honest materials.

Over the last 16 years, our 42-member interdisciplinary team of civil engineers, interior architects, landscape designers, and parametric modelers has delivered more than 85 monumental residences and corporate facilities.

Our Four Pillars

I

Bioclimatic Orientation

Every cantilevered slab and recessed courtyard is positioned according to micro-climate heat maps, cutting active air-conditioning load by up to 35%.

II

Material Authenticity

We celebrate exposed board-marked concrete, natural Kota stone, reclaimed teak wood, and hand-chiselled granite that weathers gracefully over generations.

III

Bespoke Turnkey Curation

From custom solid brass handles to hand-knotted Persian rugs and architectural lighting fixtures, every detail is engineered specifically for the client.
