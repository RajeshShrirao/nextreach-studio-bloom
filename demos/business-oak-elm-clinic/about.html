Our Story & Standards

Ethical Dental Care without the Hospital Coldness

Founded in 2014, Oak & Elm was created to redefine dental clinic visits into tranquil, reassuring wellness experiences.

Precision Technology Meets Gentle Hands

Too many patients avoid dentists because of childhood trauma, harsh drilling sounds, or cold sterile environments. At Oak & Elm, every corner was intentionally curated — acoustic fluted wood paneling, warm 3000K lighting, noise-canceling headphones, and virtually silent Japanese electric handpieces.

We employ 3D digital intraoral cameras so you can see exactly what we see on high-definition screens before deciding on any treatment. No pressure, no upselling.
